title: Fork a crew for a board (no longer offered)
summary: Forking is no longer offered. Existing forks stay until the crew is taken off the board.
requires: team-admin
topics: fork, crew, agents, keys, accounts, deprecated
order: 40
---

Forking a crew for a board is **no longer offered** in Taskmanager. To give a board its own agents, add a new crew to the
board instead: [Put an AI crew to work on this board](/help/crews/give-a-board-a-crew).

A board that already has forked members keeps them. Taking the crew off the board removes its forks and their keys.
