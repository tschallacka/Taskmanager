A board can be filed under one of your team's **customers**. The people who log in for that customer, its
**contacts**, can then open the board, but only see what you have shared with them. The rest of the board is yours
alone. This page explains how you control that, and the defaults that catch people out.

## The two settings

Every task and every comment has one of two visibilities:

| Setting | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Team only** | Your team. A lock marks it. |
| **Shared with customer** | Your team, and the contacts of the board's customer. |

A board with no customer has no outside readers, so on such a board the setting changes nothing for contacts. It does
still matter for the public link; see [Share a board by public link](/help/basics/share-a-board).

## Change a task's visibility

1. Open the task.
2. Click the button in the panel's header. It reads **Team only** with a lock, or **Shared with customer** with an
   eye. Hover for what a click does.
3. A message tells you what changed (*Now team only: the customer cannot see this task.* or *Now shared with the
   customer.*) and has an **Undo** button for a few seconds.

A Team only task shows a small lock and **Team only** on its card, so you can see at a glance what is hidden.

## The defaults

- **A new task is Shared with customer.** A task you add to a board is visible to that board's customer's contacts
  straight away, and to anyone with the board's public link. Open the task and switch it to **Team only** when it is
  internal work.
- **A new comment is Team only.** The other way round: you have to choose to tell the customer. See
  [Comments and updates](/help/basics/comments-and-updates).
- **A subtask copies its parent** when you create it.
- **A task a contact files is always shared**, so they can see what they asked for.

## What a customer's contact sees

A contact opens the same board, but:

- A Team only task does not exist for them: it is not on the board, not in lists and not reachable by its address. The
  board's counts leave it out too.
- They see the title, description, labels, assignee, dates, done mark and subtasks of shared tasks, and the shared
  comments. They cannot edit a task, move it or change its labels.
- They can add a task to a column and write comments. They cannot give a new task an assignee or a due date: the team
  decides who takes it and when.
- They do not get the **Messages**, **Time** and **Settings** tabs, custom fields, the trash, or the status updates.
- Blocked tasks show as blocked, but a blocker that is Team only reads as *a hidden task*.

Customers get their own help page about this, written from their side.

## Things to get right

- Switching a task to Team only takes it off the public link as well, and the images in its description stop being
  served publicly.
- Visibility is per task. Moving a task to another column does not change it, and a column has no visibility of its
  own.
- The comment's setting is separate from the task's: a shared comment on a Team only task still cannot be read by a
  contact, because they cannot open the task.
- Putting a board under a different customer changes who can read it. Do it on purpose.
