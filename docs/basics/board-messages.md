The **Messages** tab is where your team talks about the board as a whole: a decision, a question, a heads-up. It is
not for one task (use [comments](/help/basics/comments-and-updates) for that) and it is **never shown to customers**.
A customer's contact has no Messages tab, and the server refuses to give them the messages.

## Post a message

1. Open the board and click the **Messages** tab.
2. Optionally fill in **Subject (optional)**, up to 200 characters.
3. Write in the box under it (*Start a conversation with the team...*), up to 10,000 characters.
4. Click **Post**, or press **Ctrl+Enter** (**Cmd+Enter** on a Mac).

A message cannot be empty. The text is shown as written, with your line breaks, but it is plain text: Markdown
formatting is not drawn.

## Reply

Each conversation has a **Reply** link under it, showing how many replies it already has. Click it, write, and
press **Reply** or Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter. **Cancel** closes the box without sending.

Replies stay under the message they answer. A conversation is one message and its replies, one level deep: you cannot
reply to a reply, and a reply has no subject of its own. Conversations are listed newest first, and replies oldest
first.

## Who is told

When you post a new message everyone in the team on that board is told in their **Inbox**, except you. A reply goes to
the person who started the conversation and to the people who have already replied. Opening a notification takes the
reader straight to that one conversation, with its reply box open; **All messages** brings back the full list.

## Delete a message

Hover over a message or reply you wrote and click the bin at its right. Only the author can delete, and there is no
confirmation and no undo. **Deleting a message deletes all the replies under it too**, including other people's.

## Good to know

- Messages belong to the board. If the board is deleted, its messages go with it.
- Messages cannot be edited after posting. Delete and post again, or reply with a correction.
- Messages are the team's own talk. If a customer should see something, put it in a comment marked
  **Shared with the customer**, or in a task that is shared. See [Who sees a task](/help/basics/task-visibility).
