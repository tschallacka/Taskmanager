Some people are let onto one board as a **guest**. A guest has an account and signs in, and sees the board like a
customer's contact does: the tasks and comments the team has shared, and nothing else. See
[Working in a board shared with you](/help/basics/working-in-a-shared-board) for what that looks like.

A guest is either an **Editor** or a **Manager guest**. The team picks the role when it invites you. A manager guest may
bring more people onto the board. An editor cannot, and does not see a **Share** button. Neither does a contact of a
customer who is not a manager guest.

## Find the Share dialog

If you are a manager guest, the board has a **Share** button at the top right. Next to it, on wide screens, is a row of
avatars. Click either to open the dialog. Its title is *Share* followed by the board's name.

The dialog has two parts: **Invite with email**, and **Who has access**. You do not see the team's own settings, and you
do not choose a role: everyone you invite is an editor.

## Invite someone at your own domain

The team gave you a domain, such as `superfabulousshop.com`. The dialog reminds you: *You can invite people at ... straight
away.*

1. Type the person's email address in **Add a guest by email...**.
2. Click **Invite**.
3. The dialog shows the invitation link. If the instance can send mail, it says *Invitation mailed to ...*. Otherwise it
   says *Send this link to ...* with a **Copy** button. Send the link to that person.

The link works for that address only, and for 14 days. The person opens it, signs in or creates an account with that
exact address, and clicks **Accept invitation**. Copy the link when it appears: the dialog keeps it only until you leave the board or reload the page. If
you lose it, **Withdraw** the invitation and invite again.

The domain match is exact. If your domain is `shop.com`, then `anna@shop.com` is at your domain. `anna@mail.shop.com`
and `anna@notshop.com` are not. If the team gave you no domain, you ask for every address.

## Ask for someone at another domain

When the address is outside your domain, the dialog changes. The button reads **Ask**, and a box appears: *Why should
this person be invited?* Write a reason of at least three characters. A project admin of the team reads it and decides.

Nothing is sent to the person yet. The dialog says *Sent to the project admins. ... is invited once one of them approves
it.* The request waits under **Who has access** as *Waiting for approval*. You can **Withdraw** it.

You are told in your Inbox when the team decides: *... was approved for ...* or *... was not approved for ...*. The
approval notice says that the invitation was mailed, or points you to the **Share** dialog for the link. The dialog does
not list the link of an open invitation, so if no mail was sent and you did not copy it, ask a project admin to withdraw the invitation and invite the person again.

## What you see under Who has access

- The guests of this board, with their role as a label.
- The invitations **you** made, whether open or waiting. You do not see invitations made by others.

You cannot change anyone's role or domain, remove a guest, or approve a request. Only a project admin can.

## Notifications

The dialog has a box, **Notify me when tasks are added to the project**. Tick it to be told about each new task. You are
told only about tasks you may read.
