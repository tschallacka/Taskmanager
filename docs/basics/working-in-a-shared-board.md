You log in as a contact of a customer, or as a guest invited to a board. The team you work with has shared a board with you so you can follow the work
and talk to them about it. This page explains what you will find there.

## Find the board

The boards that are shared with you are listed in the sidebar, under **Projects**. Each row shows the board's name,
your company's name and how many tasks it holds. Click one to open it.

## What you can see

You see the tasks the team has chosen to share with you. Other tasks on the same board do not appear at all, and the
counts in the header only include what you can see.

- On the **Board** tab the tasks are laid out in columns, from first stage to last. The column a task is in tells you
  how far it has come. A circle with a tick beside a task's title means it is done.
- A card shows the title, the start of its description, labels, a due date (red when it is overdue), the number of
  comments and subtasks, and who it is assigned to.
- A card marked **Blocked** (or **Blocked by 2**) is waiting for other tasks to be finished first. You may not be able
  to see the task it waits for: it then reads as *a hidden task*.
- The **List**, **Timeline**, **Calendar**, **Dashboard** and **Files** tabs show the same tasks in other ways. You do
  not get the team's own tabs: **Messages**, **Time** and **Settings**. You also do not see the team's custom fields.
  You cannot move cards, rename columns or change labels.

Click a card to open it. The panel shows the task's column, its assignee, start and due dates, what it is waiting for,
its subtasks, its description (with any pictures, tables, charts and diagrams) and the comments. It is **read-only**:
you can look at everything in it, but not change the task itself.

In the comments area, between the comments, is the task's **activity log**: when it was created, moved, finished or
given a new date.

## Ask for something

You can add a task yourself. In a column, click **Add task**, type a short title and press Enter. The task is shared
with the team and with you from the start, so you will always be able to find it again.

You choose the title only. You cannot add a description, an assignee or a due date to a task: the team decides who takes
it and when. So write a clear title, and then put the details in a **comment** on the task. You also cannot edit the
title after you have added it; if you need a change, comment on the task and ask the team.

## Comments

Open a task and write in the box at the bottom, headed **Write to the team and the customer...**. Press **Enter** to send
and **Shift+Enter** for a new line. A little formatting works as you type: `**bold**`, `*italic*` and links.

- Your comments are shared: the team reads them, and so do other contacts of your company who can open the task.
- Under a comment from someone else you can click **Reply**. Your reply shows a line that points back to the comment
  it answers.
- You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own comments from the **...** menu on them. Deleting asks first and cannot be
  undone. You cannot change anybody else's.
- **Copy link** on a comment gives you a link to it, which you can send to a colleague at your company.

You only see comments the team chose to share, so a conversation can look shorter than it is on their side.

## Good to know

- If you cannot find a task you were told about, it may not be shared with you. Ask the team.
- The team can stop sharing a task at any time. When they do, it disappears from your board, along with its comments.
- The column a task sits in tells you how far along it is; the team moves it as the work moves on.
