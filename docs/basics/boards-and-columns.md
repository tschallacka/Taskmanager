A **board** (the app also calls it a **project**) is where one piece of work lives. It holds **columns**, the stages
the work moves through, and each column holds **tasks**, shown as cards. You move a card from column to column as the
work moves on.

## What is on a board

- **Columns** are the board's sections, such as *Backlog*, *To do*, *Verify* and *Done*. You name them, colour them,
  reorder them and delete them. See [Columns and the trash](/help/basics/columns-and-trash).
- **Tasks** are the cards. A task has a title and a description, and can carry labels, an assignee, a start and a due
  date, a done mark, subtasks and more. See [Create and edit tasks](/help/basics/tasks).
- A board belongs to your team, or to your own workspace when you are not in a team. Everyone in the team can open it.
  A board can also be filed under one of the team's customers, which is what lets that customer's contacts see the
  tasks you share. See [Who sees a task](/help/basics/task-visibility).

A new board starts with four columns: **Backlog**, **To do**, **Verify** and **Done**. If your team has chosen its own
set, new boards start with that set instead; the owners and admins of the team choose it in the team's settings. Either
way you can rename, add and remove columns at any time.

## The tabs

Under the board's name is a row of views. They are different ways of looking at the same tasks. The first five are
tabs, in this order: **Board**, **List**, **Calendar**, **Timeline** and **Dashboard**. The rest sit under **More**.

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The board's description, its health status and its status updates. Under **More**. |
| **Board** | The columns and cards. This is where a board opens. |
| **List** | The tasks as rows, one section per column, with a cell for each custom field. |
| **Calendar** | Tasks on the day they are due. Drag a task onto a day to set its due date. |
| **Timeline** | A bar for each task that has a start or a due date. |
| **Dashboard** | Counts and charts: completed, incomplete and overdue tasks, and tasks per column. |
| **Messages** | Conversations for the team only. See [Board messages](/help/basics/board-messages). Under **More**. |
| **Time** | Time logged on the board's tasks, a week at a time. Under **More**. |
| **Files** | Every image pasted into a task description on the board. Under **More**. |
| **Office** | On a crew's board: the crew of this board at work. With more than one crew, More lists each one. Under **More**. |

**More** is the last tab in the row (its button is labelled *More project views*). It holds **Overview**, **Messages**,
**Time**, **Files** and, on a crew's board, **Office**. Click it to open the list; on a phone, tap it. With the keyboard, Tab to it and press Enter to
open the list, then Enter on a view to choose it. Escape closes the list without a choice.

At the right end of the row, on the Board view, there are three things. **Filter** narrows the cards by label, by one assignee (or **Unassigned**); its list shows each label on the board with a count, and **Show deleted** puts the trash on the board. **Compact cards** in the same list hides each card's snippet and cover, so more cards fit a column; it is kept in this browser, for every board. **Search this board** narrows the cards by title, and an empty result says so. **New task** opens the composer in the first column. On a phone the tabs scroll sideways; Filter, search and New task stay in view.

The top bar shows the board's team and name (**Team › Project**; click the project name to rename it), the board's
health (**On track**, **At risk** or **Off track**, when it has one, set on **Overview**), and the people on the board
as avatars: the first four, then a count. **Share** and the cog are on the right. The cog is **Settings**. The **...**
menu (*Project options*) next to it holds *Rename project*, *Settings* and, when something has been deleted, *Show
trashed*.

## Moving tasks without a mouse

Dragging a card or a bar is the quick way to move it. The same moves are open to the keyboard and to a tap:

- **Calendar.** Each task in the calendar has a **Move to another day** control. Press it (Enter with the keyboard, a
  tap on a phone) to pick the task; the picked task is marked. Then press an arrow key: Left and Right move its due day
  by a day, Up and Down by a week. On a phone, tap a day to put the picked task there. Undated tasks are listed under
  *No date*, and the same control sets their day.
- **Timeline.** Focus a bar and press an arrow key: Left and Right move the whole bar a day. Hold Shift with an arrow to
  change only its due date, or Alt with an arrow to change only its start date. While a bar has focus, Left and Right
  (Alt included) belong to the bar, so the browser's Back and Forward do not run and the page stays put. On a phone, or with a mouse, use the
  row's **Move the bar** menu: *Move one day earlier*, *Move one day later*, *End one day earlier* and *End one day later*.
  The two *End* items change the due date only. Each item gives the same dates as its key.

## On a phone

On a phone the app has a tab bar at the bottom: **Home**, **Inbox** (with the number of unread items), **My tasks**,
**Projects** and **Search**. The round **+** above it creates a new project. The drawer menu is still there for the rest.

On a board, the phone shows one column at a time. The **column picker** above the board is a row of chips, one per
column, each with its number of tasks; tap one to see its cards. A sideways **swipe** on the board moves to the next or
the previous column (the caption *swipe for other columns* is under the picker). The last chip, **Add column**, creates a
column: on a phone the name field shows under the picker. Each card has a **⋯** button at its top right. A long-press on a
card opens the same sheet. The sheet has **Mark done**, **Move to** (the column chips), **Assign to**, **Set due date** and
**Delete**. Delete moves the task to the trash and shows an **Undo** for a few seconds. A trashed task can be restored from *Show trashed* for 7 days. On a wide screen the
board shows every column side by side, and the **⋯** button is not there: use the card's menu instead.

## The List tab

The List tab shows the board's tasks as rows, one section per column (a section collapses when you click its name).
Above the rows:

- **Group by** puts the rows in their columns, or one section per person (*Assignee*).
- **Sort** orders each section by due date, or by title.
- **Columns** shows or hides *Assignee*, *Due*, *Labels* and *Time* for the board. Your choice is remembered in this
  browser, for this board.

Most cells change in place, with no need to open the task:

- Click a task's **title** to rename it. Press Enter, or click elsewhere, to save; Escape keeps the old title.
- Click the **person** to change the assignee, or to choose *Unassigned*.
- Pick a **date** in the due cell. Clear it to remove the due date.
- Click the **labels** to type them, separated by commas.
- Click **+ time** (or the logged time) to log time on the task. Type `1h 30m`, `1:30` or `45m`.

On a phone the same cells sit under the title of each row, so nothing is left out.

## The numbers in the header

The header shows something like **8/47 tasks**: 8 open of 47 in total. Hover it for the words. "Open" is worked out
from the columns, not from the done mark:

- A column counts as an **ending** column when its name contains *done*, *shipped*, *complete*, *closed*, *live*,
  *cancel*, *reject*, *dropped* or *archive*. Mind that it is a text match: a column called *Delivery* counts, because
  it contains "live".
- When no column matches, the last column is the ending one.
- A board with a single column has no ending, so it shows the plain total.

Tasks in an ending column are not counted as open. The same count is shown under each board's name in the sidebar.
The *All projects* page counts differently: it counts a task as open until it has the **done mark**.

## Good to know

- Changes made by teammates, by an agent or by the AI assistant show up on an open board without a reload.
- A board you open from a crew can say *This is a crew's board. You can read it here; only the crew's team changes
  it.* You can look but not change anything on it.
- There is no separate permission for boards. Everyone in the team, not only owners and admins, may create, rename,
  archive and delete a board.
- Read [Create and manage a board](/help/basics/create-and-manage-a-board) to make one, or
  [Run a small project from start to finish](/help/basics/run-a-small-project) for the whole route in one go.
