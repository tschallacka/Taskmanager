This walkthrough follows one made-up project, **Website relaunch**, from an empty board to an archived one. Follow it
with your own project name. It takes about 20 minutes and touches nearly everything in the Basics pages.

## 1. Create the board

In the sidebar, click the **+** next to **Projects**, type *Website relaunch* and click **Create**. The board opens with
four columns: **Backlog**, **To do**, **Verify** and **Done**. See
[Create and manage a board](/help/basics/create-and-manage-a-board).

Open the **Overview** tab. Under **Project description** write the goal and a link or two. Click **On track** under
**What's the status?**, write *Kick-off done, scope agreed* and click **Post update**. That is the first entry in the
board's history.

## 2. Shape the columns

Back on the **Board** tab, you want a stage for work that waits for the client. Click **Add column** at the right, type
*Client review* and press Enter. Drag its header between **Verify** and **Done**. Hover over the column and use its
**...** menu, **Change color**, to give it a colour. See [Columns and the trash](/help/basics/columns-and-trash).

## 3. Add the first tasks

In **Backlog**, click **Add task** and type a title, then Enter, and keep going:

- *Collect brand assets*
- *Design the home page*
- *Build the home page*
- *Write the launch announcement*

Press Escape when you are done. A task is only a title so far. See [Create and edit tasks](/help/basics/tasks).

## 4. Fill in a task

Open **Design the home page**. Give it the label *design*, pick an **Assignee**, and set a **Start date** and a **Due
date**. In the description write a short brief, and paste a screenshot of the old home page straight into it: the image
is uploaded and kept with the task. Try **/** and choose **Mermaid diagram** to sketch the page flow; see
[Write in the editor](/help/basics/the-editor) and [Code blocks, charts and diagrams](/help/basics/code-blocks-and-diagrams).

Add three **Subtasks**: *Wireframe*, *Visual design*, *Mobile version*. Press Enter after each. See
[Subtasks, blockers and custom fields](/help/basics/subtasks-blockers-and-fields).

## 5. Say what waits for what

Open **Build the home page**. Under **Add a task this one waits for** type *Design* and click **Design the home page**.
The card now carries the chip **Blocked by 1**. When the design task is finished, the chip goes away.

In **Settings**, **Custom fields**, click **Priority**. Every task now has a Priority dropdown; set it to High on the
two tasks that matter most.

## 6. Move the work along

Drag **Design the home page** into **To do** when you start it. When you finish, tick its circle to mark it done, and
drag the card to **Client review**. Its subtasks go with it. Remember that the circle and the column are separate: the
tick says finished, the column says where it is.

## 7. Talk about it

Open the task and write a comment: *Waiting for the client's logo in SVG.* It starts as **Team only**, which is what
you want for a note to yourself. See [Comments and updates](/help/basics/comments-and-updates). For a decision that
concerns the whole board, post it in the **Messages** tab instead: [Board messages](/help/basics/board-messages).

## 8. Bring in the client

The client should be able to follow along. In the header click **Team only** and choose the client from the list
(your team's owner or admin adds customers under **Team**). The board is now filed under that client.

Before you leave it like that, go through the tasks: new tasks start as **Shared with customer**, so the client now sees
all of them. Open any task that is internal, such as one with pricing notes, and click its **Shared with customer**
button so it says **Team only**. Read [Who sees a task](/help/basics/task-visibility), then write a comment and click the
lock beside the box so it turns into an eye (**Shared with the customer**) before you send it.

If you would rather give a stakeholder a link than an account, use **Share**; see
[Share a board by public link](/help/basics/share-a-board).

## 9. Keep the board honest

Delete a task you added by mistake: **...**, **Delete task**. Click **Undo** in the message, or later choose **...**
then **Show trashed** and **Restore** it. Change the status to **At risk** on the Overview and post an update whenever
things change.

## 10. Finish

When everything is in **Done**, set the status, post a last update, and open **Settings**, **General**. Click
**Archive project**. The board leaves the project lists, its rules stop, and you can still open it from **Browse
projects**, under **Archived**, and click **Unarchive** to bring it back. Do not click **Delete project** unless you
mean it: that one cannot be undone.
