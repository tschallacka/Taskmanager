Columns are the stages of a board. This page covers changing them, and what happens to the work in them when a
column or a task is deleted. Deleting here is safe for a week: deleted columns and tasks wait in the board's **trash**.

## Add a column

At the right end of the board click **Add column**, type a name and press Enter. Clicking
away with a name typed also adds it, and so does pressing Escape: Escape closes the box but keeps the name you typed.
To back out, clear the name first. The new column lands at the far right; drag it where you want it.

## Rename, recolour and reorder

Hover over a column's header to see its buttons. The **...** (*Column options*) menu has:

- **Add task**: starts a new card in this column.
- **Rename**: or just click the column's name, type, and press Enter.
- **Change color**: pick one of the palette colours for the dot in the header, or **Automatic** to let the dot follow
  the column's name again.
- **Delete column**.

To reorder columns, drag a column by its header and drop it between two others. On a touchscreen, press and hold the
header for a moment first, so an ordinary swipe still scrolls the board.

The number beside a column's name is how many tasks it holds. A column with more than 20 tasks shows the first 20
and folds the rest into a button, for example **+12 older**; click it to open the whole column. Adding a task to the
column opens it too.

## Delete a column

1. Open the column's **...** menu and choose **Delete column**.
2. Read the window and click **Delete column**.

What happens to its tasks:

- They **move to the previous column**. If you delete the first column, they move to the next one instead.
- The column itself goes to the board's trash.
- If it is the board's **only** column, the tasks have nowhere to go, so they go to the trash with it. The window
  says so and the button then reads **Delete column and N tasks**.

A message says *"Name" moved to trash* and reminds you that it can be restored for the next 7 days.

## Show and restore what was deleted

Once anything is in the trash, the board's **...** menu (*Project options*) gets an entry **Show trashed (N)**. Choose
it and the deleted columns and cards appear on the board, in the place they had, faded. A bar says how many deleted
items are shown, and a small line on each says when it was deleted and, if it was not you at the keyboard, who did:
*by an agent over MCP* or *by the AI assistant*. Click **Hide** (or **Hide trashed** in the menu) to close the view.

- On a deleted **task**, click **Restore**. It goes back to the column and place it had. If that column is in the trash
  too, the column comes back with it, and a message names it.
- On a deleted **column**, click **Restore**. The tasks that went down with it come back, and so do the tasks that
  were handed to a neighbour, unless you have moved those somewhere on purpose since. A message says how many came
  back.
- The red bin on a deleted card or column is **Delete permanently**. It asks first, and after it there is nothing to
  restore.

A deleted task is not shown in search and does not count in the board's totals. You cannot edit a deleted task or
column; restore it first.

## Deleting tasks

In a task's panel, open the **...** menu (*Task options*) and choose **Delete task**, then **Delete task** again. The
task goes to the trash and a message with an **Undo** button stays for 10 seconds. A subtask is different: it is
removed for good the moment you remove it, with no trash and no undo. See
[Subtasks, blockers and custom fields](/help/basics/subtasks-blockers-and-fields).

## The trash empties itself

Anything that has been in the trash for more than 7 days is removed automatically, and then it cannot be restored.
The trash belongs to the team: customers' contacts never see it. To remove a whole board see
[Create and manage a board](/help/basics/create-and-manage-a-board); a deleted board has no trash.
