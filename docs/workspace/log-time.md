This page is the steps for recording time. For how time works and who sees it, read
[Time tracking](/help/workspace/time-tracking).

## Time a task with the timer

1. Open the task: click its card, or open it from **My tasks** or the search box.
2. In the task panel, find the **Time** section near the bottom. Click **Start timer**.
3. The button turns red and reads **Stop** with a clock, for example `Stop 00:12:40`. The same timer now shows in the
   sidebar, under the workspace links, with the task's title.
4. Work. You can leave the task, change page, or close the tab: the timer keeps running.
5. When you are done, click **Stop** in the task panel, or the square **Stop timer** button on the sidebar timer.

The time is logged at once as an entry on that task, and the task's **Time** line shows the new total, for example
**1h 30m logged**.

You can only have one timer. If one is running on another task, the button's hover text warns you: **Starting this
timer stops the one running on another task**. Clicking it does stop the first timer and logs that time, then starts
the new one.

If it goes wrong you get **Could not change the timer** or **Could not stop the timer**. Try once more; check the
sidebar to see whether the timer still shows before you try again.

## Log time by hand on a task

Use this when you forgot to start a timer, or the work happened somewhere else.

1. Open the task and go to its **Time** section.
2. In the first box, type how long it took, such as `1h 30m`, `1:30` or `45m`.
3. Check the date. It starts as today. Change it for work done on another day.
4. Optionally type what it was in **What was it?**. A note can hold up to 500 characters.
5. Click **Add**. The button stays off until you have typed a duration.

If the duration is not one the app understands you see **Write the time like 1h 30m, 1:30 or 45m** under the form.
The ways to write a duration, and the limits (one minute to 24 hours per entry), are on the
[Time tracking](/help/workspace/time-tracking) page.

The entry appears in the list under the form with its day (**Today** or the date), its length, who logged it and your
note.

## Log time by hand from the Timesheet

The Timesheet lets you add time without opening the task first.

1. Open **Timesheet** in the sidebar.
2. In the row under the grid, choose a task in **Pick a task…**. The tasks are grouped by project.
3. Type the length, set the day, and add a note if you like.
4. Click **Add time**.

If you forget to pick a task you see **Pick the task the time was spent on**. When it works, the length and the note
are cleared, ready for the next entry, and the grid updates.

The list of tasks only holds tasks that are not completed, in projects you work on as staff. To log time on a
completed task, open that task and use its own **Time** section.

## Delete an entry

You can delete only entries you logged.

1. Find the entry: in the task's **Time** list, or in your **Timesheet** under its day.
2. Hover it and click the trash button (**Delete entry**).

It goes at once, without asking, and it cannot be undone. Entries have no edit, so to correct one, delete it and add
it again with the right values.

If it cannot be deleted you see **Could not delete the entry**.

## Good to know

- The days you pick are calendar days. Time you log for yesterday lands on yesterday's column of the Timesheet.
- A timer you stop in the app is logged on the day you stop it.
- Someone else may have logged time on the same task. Their entries show in the task's list with their name, but you
  cannot delete them.

See your week on the [Timesheet](/help/workspace/timesheet), or follow [Track a day of work](/help/workspace/track-a-day-of-work)
from start to finish.
