A **goal** is an outcome you want, such as "Launch the new site". Projects are how you get there. A goal ties the
projects together, shows whether it is on track, and reports how far along it is.

## Who sees goals

Goals belong to the workspace you are in. In a team, everyone on the team sees the team's goals and any member can
change or delete them: the server does not check a role for goals. In your Personal Workspace only you see yours.
Switch workspace to see another set.

## Add a goal

1. Open **Goals** in the sidebar.
2. Type a name in the box ("Name the goal, such as “Launch the new site”"). **Add goal** stays off until there is
   text.
3. Press **Add goal**. The new goal opens so you can fill in the rest.

You are recorded as the goal's owner, and the owner's name shows under the title.

## What a goal holds

Click a goal to open it. Click it again to close it.

- **Title**: edit it in place; it saves when you leave the field.
- **Status**: **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track**, **Achieved** or **Dropped**. Click the status to set it,
  click the same one again to clear it. A goal with none says **No status**.
- **Due**: a date.
- **Progress**: a slider from 0 to 100 in steps of 5.
- **Read progress from the linked projects**: tick it and the goal reports the average of how much of each linked
  project is done. The slider switches off, because the number is now worked out for you.
- **Projects that move this goal**: a checklist of the workspace's projects. Tick the ones that count toward the
  goal. Only projects of your current workspace can be linked.

The list row shows the status, the owner, the due date, how many projects are linked, and a progress bar.

How done a project is comes from its top-level tasks: a task is finished when it is marked done or sits in a
finishing column, and the project's percentage is the share of finished tasks. A project with no tasks counts as 0%.

## Sub-goals

A big goal can be broken up. Open a top-level goal and use **Add a sub-goal** at the bottom, then **Add**. Sub-goals
are listed beneath their goal, indented, and have the same fields. They go one level deep: a sub-goal does not offer
**Add a sub-goal**.

## Delete a goal

1. Open the goal and click **Delete goal**.
2. Confirm **Delete** in **Delete this goal?**.

This cannot be undone. The goal's sub-goals stay, and move to the top of the list. The linked projects are not
touched.

## When something goes wrong

- If a change is refused you get a message such as **Could not save the goal**, with the server's reason when it has
  one.
- The project list only offers projects you work on as staff in the current workspace. A project you only reach as a
  customer's contact is not offered, and the list says **There are no projects yet.** when the workspace has none.
- The page updates when a teammate or an agent changes a goal, so a number can move while you look at it.

Portfolios are the other way to read several projects together. See [Portfolios](/help/workspace/portfolios).
