There are two places to read recorded time. The **Timesheet** is about you: your own time, across every project. The
**Time** tab on a project is about the project: everyone's time on it. Both read one week at a time, Monday to Sunday.

To record time, see [Start a timer and log time](/help/workspace/log-time).

## The Timesheet

Open **Timesheet** in the sidebar. It is private to you: it lists only entries you logged. It follows you, not the
workspace you happen to have open, so it shows your time on every project you logged on, in any team or in your
Personal Workspace.

### Pick the week

At the top:

- **Previous week** and **Next week** (the arrow buttons) move one week at a time.
- The label in the middle shows the dates of the week you are reading, for example `Mar 2 – Mar 8, 2026`.
- **This week** appears when you are looking at another week, and jumps back.
- On the right, the total for the week, such as `12h 30m this week`.

### The grid

Under the header is a grid: one row for each task you logged time on that week, and one column for each day, Monday
to Sunday. Today's column is highlighted. A dot means nothing was logged that day. The last column is the total for the
task, the last row is the total for each day, and the bottom right corner is the total for the week. Rows are ordered
with the most time first, and each shows its project under the task title.

With no entries, the grid says **No time logged this week.**

### Add time

The row below the grid is a form for adding time by hand. Its steps are in
[Start a timer and log time](/help/workspace/log-time).

### The entries, day by day

Under the form the same week is listed day by day, newest day first. Each day has a heading with its date (**Today**
for today) and the day's total. Each entry shows:

- its length;
- the task title;
- the project, on wider screens;
- your note, on wider screens.

Hover an entry and click the trash button to delete it. This cannot be undone.

The page refreshes by itself when a timer stops, and when time is logged elsewhere, by you in another tab, or by an
agent using your key.

## A project's Time tab

To see what a whole project has cost:

1. Open the project's board.
2. Click the **Time** tab in the row of views.
3. Use the arrow buttons to choose the week. **This week** jumps back to the current one.

You get two grids for the week, each with the same days and totals as the Timesheet:

- **By person**: one row for each person who logged time on the project that week. It answers "who worked on this?"
- **By task**: one row for each task, with the same time added up across people. It answers "what did the time go on?"

The total at the top, for example `4h 15m this week`, is everyone's time on the project that week, and reads `0m` when
nothing was logged.

Everyone on the project team sees this tab, so everyone on the team can see how much each person logged. If that is not
what you want from your time, tell your team before you log it: there is no private option on a project.

## Limits

- The grid shows one week at a time. To compare weeks, move between them. The server answers for at most 92 days at a
  time, and a week is well inside that.
- Up to 2,000 entries are read for a week. A week with more than that would show only the newest.
- Neither page totals time per customer. If a customer has several boards, read each board's Time tab in turn.
