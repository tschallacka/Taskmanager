Two things help you get to a board quickly: **Browse projects**, which lists every project of your workspace, and
**pins**, which keep chosen boards at the top of the sidebar.

## Browse projects

Open **Browse projects** in the sidebar. It lists the projects of the workspace you are in, newest change first.

1. Type in **Find a project** to narrow the list. It matches on the project's name.
2. Read the row: the project's name, a coloured dot when the project has a health status (green on track, orange at
   risk, red off track), **Open** (tasks not marked done), **Total** (all its tasks), and **Updated** (how long ago
   it last changed, shown on wider screens).
3. Click a row to open the board.

**No projects match.** means your words match nothing. Check the workspace in the team switcher: the list is only
the projects of the workspace you are in.

The **Open** number here counts tasks not marked done. The count in the sidebar is a little different: it counts
tasks that have not reached a finishing column of the board. The two can disagree, and neither is wrong.

### Archived projects

An archived project leaves every list, so **Browse projects** is the one place to find it again. When there are any,
an **Archived** section appears under the list. Its text explains the effect: archived boards still open, and their
rules do not run until they are unarchived.

To bring one back, click **Unarchive** on its row. It returns to the lists at once and its rules run again. If it
cannot be restored you see the reason under the list.

## Pin a board

A pin is a shortcut that belongs to you. Nobody else sees your pins, and pinning changes nothing about the board.

1. Hover a project in the sidebar and click the **Project options** button (the three dots).
2. Choose **Pin to sidebar**.

The board now shows under **Pinned**, above the project list. Each pinned board shows its name and where it lives:
the team name, or **Personal Workspace**, and the customer's name when it has one.

- Click a pinned board to open it. If it is in another workspace, Taskmanager switches to that workspace first, the
  same way the team switcher does.
- To remove it, hover the pinned board and click the unpin button, or open **Project options** on the project and
  choose **Unpin**.

Pins follow you across workspaces: one list shows boards from your Personal Workspace and from every team you are in.
A board drops out of your pins when it is archived or you can no longer open it, for example after you leave the team.
If you are added back, you pin it again.

Anyone may pin a board they can open, including a customer's contact. The other items in that menu (such as renaming
or deleting a project) depend on what you may do with the board.
