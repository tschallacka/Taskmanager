An account can have more than one email address. The first one is your main address. Each extra address must be confirmed before it counts for anything.

## Before you add one

Turn on two-factor authentication, or add a passkey, in **Settings** → **Security**. An account with an extra address needs one of them, so an extra address never becomes a way round them. While an extra address is on the account, you cannot turn two-factor off or remove your last passkey. Remove the extra addresses first.

## Add an address and confirm it

1. In **Settings** → **Security**, type the address under **Extra email addresses** and choose **Add address**.
2. We send a confirmation link to that address. It is valid for 24 hours.
3. Open the link while you are signed in to the account that added the address. If you are signed out, sign in first and open the link again.

The address shows **Waiting for its confirmation link** until then, and it does nothing for you yet. Adding the same address again sends a new link and replaces the waiting one. A link works once.

An address that is the main address of another account, or a confirmed extra address of one, is refused with "That address is already in use." A waiting address of another account does not block you.

## Sign in with it

Type the extra address and your password on the sign-in page. The password is that of your account, so the same password works for both addresses. Signing in with an email code is not offered for an account with extra addresses: use your password or a passkey.

## Invitations

A team or board invitation sent to a confirmed extra address can be accepted by your account, while you are signed in to it. A waiting address does not match. Being already on a team counts an extra address too, so an invitation to it does not make a second account.

## Remove an address

Choose the bin icon beside the address. It is removed at once, and you can no longer sign in with it.

Other people do not see your extra addresses.
