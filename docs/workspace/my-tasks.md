**My tasks** gathers the tasks assigned to you from every project of the workspace you are in, so you do not have to
visit each board. It shows top-level tasks only: a subtask lives in the panel of the task it belongs to.

Open it from **My tasks** in the sidebar. At the top right, **list** and **board** switch between the two views. The
choice is kept in the page address, so a reload or a shared link shows the same view.

## The list

Three tabs split your tasks:

- **Upcoming**: open tasks that are not overdue, including tasks with no due date.
- **Overdue**: open tasks whose due date has passed. The tab shows how many, for example **Overdue (3)**.
- **Completed**: tasks marked done.

Inside a tab, tasks are sorted by due date, soonest first, and tasks without a date come last. Each row shows:

- a circle: click it to mark the task complete. On a completed task it reads **Mark incomplete** and puts the task
  back to open;
- the task title (struck through when it is done);
- the project it belongs to;
- the due date, in red when it is overdue.

Click anywhere else on the row to open the task on its board, in its panel.

An empty tab says **Nothing in upcoming.** (or overdue, or completed).

## The board

The board arranges your open tasks by when they are due, not by stage:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Overdue** | Past due |
| **Today** | Due today |
| **Upcoming** | Due in the next 7 days |
| **Later** | Due after that |
| **No date** | Not scheduled |

Completed tasks are not drawn. Click a card to open the task.

### Reschedule by dragging

Drag a card to another column and its due date changes to match:

- **Today** sets the due date to today.
- **Upcoming** sets it to 7 days from today.
- **Later** sets it to 14 days from today.
- **No date** removes the due date.

**Overdue** is not a place you can drop into: you cannot schedule a task into the past from here. A card dropped on
the column it already sits in is left as it is.

Those dates are a starting point. Open the task and set the exact day if 7 days is not what you meant.

## What counts as yours

A task shows up here when it is assigned to you. Tasks you only created, or only commented on, do not. The page reads
the workspace you are in, so a team's tasks appear while you are in that team, and your Personal Workspace's tasks
appear while you are there. Use the team switcher to look at another workspace. If you log in as a customer's contact,
you see only the tasks that were shared with customers.
