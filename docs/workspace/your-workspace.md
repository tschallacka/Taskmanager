Everything you do in Taskmanager starts from the **sidebar** on the left. On a phone it opens with the panel button
at the top of a page. This page explains what is in it, and what the **Home** and **Profile** pages show.

## Which workspace you are in

You always work in one workspace at a time: your **Personal Workspace**, or a team you belong to. The team switcher
near the bottom of the sidebar changes it. The workspace decides which projects, tasks, goals and portfolios you see,
so if something you expect is missing, check the switcher first.

## The sidebar, top to bottom

- **New project** (the board button next to the logo) and the **Search tasks and boards** field. See
  [Search tasks and boards](/help/workspace/search).
- The workspace links: **Home**, **Inbox** (with a count of what is unread), **My tasks**, **Browse projects**,
  **Goals**, **Portfolios**, **Timesheet** and **Profile**.
- A running timer, if you have one.
- **Pinned**: boards you chose to keep close. See [Browse projects and pin boards](/help/workspace/browse-projects-and-pins).
- **Projects**: every project of the workspace you are in, newest change first. Each row shows the customer (if the
  project has one), when it last changed, and how many tasks it holds.
- The team switcher, and under it your account menu: **Settings**, **Help**, **Dark mode** or **Light mode**, and
  **Sign out**. See [Settings](/help/workspace/settings).

## Home

Home is the page to land on to see what is yours today.

- A greeting with your first name and today's date.
- Four numbers: **Due today**, **Overdue** (red when there is any), **Done this week** (since Monday), and **Timer**,
  which names the task of the timer you have running, or says **None running**. The numbers count only the tasks
  assigned to you.
- **Getting started**, for a new person: five steps (create a project, add a task, finish one, invite someone, let an
  agent help) with a progress bar. The first three mark themselves from your work. **Invite someone** and **Let an
  agent help** are marked when you follow their link, on that browser only. **Hide** removes the card; it also leaves
  on its own when all five are done.
- **My tasks**: your open tasks, in three groups by due date: **Overdue**, **Today**, then **Later** (tasks with no
  date are in Later). Click the circle to mark a task complete, or click the row to open the task on its board.
  **View all** opens [My tasks](/help/workspace/my-tasks).
- **Inbox**: how many items are unread, and the latest three. Click one to open it. **View all** opens the inbox.
- **Projects**: your six most recently changed projects. Each shows its health (on track, at risk or off track, when
  the project has one), a progress bar of its done tasks, how many are done, and when it last changed. The member
  avatars of a project card come in a later change (card mwd39fc3xrw3et97ubg). **Browse** opens
  the full list. The dashed **New project** row starts a new project.

If nothing is assigned to you, the card says **Nothing assigned to you.**

## Profile

Profile is a page about you. It shows your picture, name and email, and three cards:

- **My tasks**: your next eight open tasks, with their project and due date.
- **Frequent collaborators**: the other people on your ten most recently changed projects. Hover a name or picture
  for the [person card](/help/basics/person-card-and-profiles). When there are none it tells you to invite teammates
  under Team, Members, or to share a board with a guest.
- **My recent projects**: your eight most recently changed projects.

The link **Edit profile in settings** takes you to [Settings](/help/workspace/settings). The Profile page itself
changes nothing.

## If you log in as a customer's contact

You see the same sidebar, but your work is smaller. The boards your customer shares with you appear in your Personal
Workspace, and you see only the tasks that were shared with customers. **Goals**, **Portfolios** and **Timesheet**
are the team's own tools and are not meant for a contact; you have no use for them.
<!-- /requires -->

<!-- requires: staff -->
## For the team

Staff also have the pages for [Goals](/help/workspace/goals), [Portfolios](/help/workspace/portfolios) and the
[Timesheet](/help/workspace/timesheet), and a running timer shows in the sidebar while you track time. See
[Time tracking](/help/workspace/time-tracking).
