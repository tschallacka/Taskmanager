The search box finds tasks and projects without you having to remember which board they are on. It works from every
page.

## Open it

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac). It works on every page, even while you are typing in an editor.
- Or click **Search tasks and boards…** at the top of the sidebar.

Close it with **Esc**. (**Cmd+Shift+K** is not this shortcut: that is the editor's link shortcut.)

## Before you type

The box lists your **Recent tasks**: the six tasks that changed most recently. Press **Enter** to open the
highlighted one.

## Searching

Type some words. Results come in two groups:

- **Tasks**: up to five. Each shows the title, a snippet of the description around your words, its labels, its
  project and when it last changed.
- **Projects**: up to three, matched by name.

Words are matched against task titles, task descriptions and project names, with titles and project names counting
for more. A half-typed word still matches (typing `sani` finds `sanitair`), and a small typo is tolerated. If nothing
matches, the box says **Nothing found for** followed by what you typed.

Use the arrow keys to move through the results and **Enter** to open the highlighted one. A task opens on its board
in its panel; a project opens its board.

## Filter by tag

Type `#` and a word to filter. A tag here is a task label, or a word from a board's name, so `#sanitairkamer` finds the
work of a board called Sanitairkamer.

- A suggestion row appears under the box as you type. Press **Tab** to complete the first suggestion.
- You do not have to finish the word: `#sani` already filters by everything it could still become.
- Several tags narrow the search: a task has to match every one of them.
- Tags alone work too. With no words, you get the tasks and projects the tags allow, newest first.

For example, `#urgent invoice` finds tasks labelled urgent that mention an invoice.

## What it searches

The search reads the tasks and projects of the workspace you are in, so switch workspace with the team switcher to
search another. It does not find:

- subtasks, which live inside their parent task;
- tasks in the trash;
- archived projects. Find those under **Browse projects**.

A customer's contact finds only the tasks that were shared with customers.

## When a result seems missing

The search list is kept in your browser and refreshed as things change. If a task someone just created is not there,
give it a moment, or reload the page. Make sure you are in the workspace that holds the task.
