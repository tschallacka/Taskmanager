You are a **contact** of a customer: someone from the other side of a project, invited to follow the work and talk
to the team about it. You sign in with your own account and see only the boards that belong to your customer.

## Accept an invitation

A person on the team sends you an invitation link. Nothing is emailed by the app, so look wherever they sent it.

1. Open the link. The page says "You're invited" and names the team and the customer.
2. If you are not signed in, click **Sign in or create an account**. You come back to the same page afterwards.
3. Click **Accept invitation**.

You land on the most recently updated of your boards, with all of them listed in the sidebar. The link works for one person and for 14 days, and the first account that accepts it
uses it up. If you see "This invitation link is not valid." or "This invitation has already been used or has
expired.", ask the team for a new link.

## Find your projects

Your customer's boards are in your **personal workspace**, the one you are in by default. Each board shows your
customer's name beside it. Open one to see its tasks.

## What you see

You see the tasks and comments the team has **shared with you**. The team keeps some tasks and comments for itself,
and you do not see those at all: not on the board, not in search, and not by a link to them. If something you
expect is missing, the team may not have shared it yet. Ask them.

A board shows you views such as Overview, Board, List, Timeline and Calendar. It has no Messages, Time or
Settings tab: those are the team's own.

## What you can do

- **Read** shared tasks, their descriptions, and shared comments.
- **Comment** on a shared task and **reply** to a shared comment. What you write is always visible to the team
  and to you.
- **Edit or delete your own comments.**
- **Add a task** where the board offers it. A task you add is shared with the team and you; the team decides who
  takes it and when, so you cannot pick an assignee or a due date yourself.

The team can also assign a task to you, which shows you as its assignee.

## What you cannot do

- Change a task, including one you added yourself: edit, move, complete or delete it.
- Change who sees a task or comment, or edit or delete someone else's comment.
- See the team's other boards, its Team page or its internal notes. You do see the name of the team members who
  work on your boards. Their email address you see only if they chose to share it as far as guests.
- Rename the board, or file it under another customer.

## If you lose access

The team can end your access at any time. You keep your account, but the boards disappear from your list. If that
was not meant, ask the team for a new invitation link.
