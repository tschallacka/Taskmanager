- **Privacy policy and legal notice pages, with their archive.** `/privacy` and `/imprint` show the current legal document, public, with its version and dates at the top. A draft, or a document with an open placeholder, is not published: the page and the API answer 404 and the server logs why. `/privacy/archive` and `/imprint/archive` list every version that was in force, newest first, with the dates from each document's front matter, and each archived version reads as it was.
