- **Invite by email, part 2: the Share dialog invites in one step, and a pending contact or invitation can be revoked.** The Share dialog's customer picker is the app's own select and opens on the customer the project is filed under, and a project can be shared with several customers. A customer's invitation link can be revoked and then stops working, a team or board invitation to an address is accepted at sign-in as a customer one is, and a withdrawn board guest link says so. Staff keep the customer's name on every team project they belong to, and the invite addresses, the Customers popup and the Share line fit a 390 px phone. Schema changes, both additive, to be applied by the owner: a new table `customer_projects` (one row per customer and project), and a new nullable column `customer_invitations.revoked_at`.
