- **A project can be shared with several customers.** Filing a project under a customer no longer refuses a project that already sits under another customer: it is shared with both, and each customer's contacts see it. The first customer stays on the project. Schema change: a new table `customer_projects` (one row per customer and project, unique on the pair), to be applied by the owner. Visibility reads (the project filters, shared with me, the invitation and accept landing lists, the pending accept list, the key's board filter and the task attachments) go through it, and projects filed before it keep being shared. - **A customer's invitation link can be revoked, and a revoked link stops working.** Revoke on a pending contact in the Customers popup keeps the link's row and sets when it was revoked; the invite page says the link was revoked, and accepting it or a later sign-in does not claim it. Schema change: a new nullable column `customer_invitations.revoked_at`, additive, to ship with the release. - **A team invitation sent to an address is accepted at sign-in, like a board or customer one.** A person who signs in with a verified address that a team invited gets the team as a member on the projects page, with no second step. A revoked or used invitation is not claimed. Files: server/utils/pendingInvites.ts, server/api/pending-invitations/claim.post.ts, tests/team-invite-claim-db.test.ts.
