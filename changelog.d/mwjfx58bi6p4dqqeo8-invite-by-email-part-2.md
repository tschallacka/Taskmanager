- **A project can be shared with several customers.** Filing a project under a customer no longer refuses a project that already sits under another customer: it is shared with both, and each customer's contacts see it. The first customer stays on the project. Schema change: a new table `customer_projects` (one row per customer and project, unique on the pair), to be applied by the owner. Visibility reads (the project filters, shared with me, the invitation and accept landing lists, the pending accept list, the key's board filter and the task attachments) go through it, and projects filed before it keep being shared.
- **A customer's invitation link can be revoked, and a revoked link stops working.** Revoke on a pending contact in the Customers popup keeps the link's row and sets when it was revoked; the invite page says the link was revoked, and accepting it or a later sign-in does not claim it. Schema change: a new nullable column `customer_invitations.revoked_at`, additive, to ship with the release.
- **A team invitation sent to an address is accepted at sign-in, like a board or customer one.** A person who signs in with a verified address that a team invited gets the team as a member on the projects page, with no second step. A revoked or used invitation is not claimed. The project Share dialog's customer control is headed "Invite a customer contact" and says only a project of a team can have a customer.
- **The invite page shows a loading line at once.** While an invitation loads, the page says "Loading your invitation…" next to the spinner, so a slow start does not look blank.
- **The Share dialog's customer picker is the app's own select.** It is AppSelect, like the other pickers in the app, with the same choice: "Choose the customer", then the team's customers. Files: `app/components/BoardShare.vue`.
- **Customer names in the Customers popup are not cut on a phone.** A long name wraps onto two lines and the counts go under it, so the rows can be told apart at 390 px.
- **Pending invite addresses wrap on a phone, and the Share line shows only with a link.** A pending team invite, a pending customer contact and an invitation link show the whole address at 390 px. The Share dialog no longer prints "For . It works for that address only." before a link exists.
- **Invite addresses wrap at word breaks on a phone, and the team invite field and the Customers title fit.** Addresses wrap without splitting words; the team invite email field takes a full row at 390 px; the Customers popup title leaves room for its close button.
- **Staff keep the customer's name on every team project they belong to.** The project list decides staff by team membership, not by the team open at the moment, so a member who has another team open sees the same customer name as on that team's projects. A contact still sees only their own customer's name. Files: `shared/utils/projectAudience.ts`, `server/api/projects.ts`, `tests/project-audience-membership.test.ts`.
