- **Crew kit: a board outage is waited out, not a reason to stop.** The listener retries a board call with backoff (60 s, doubling, capped at 5 min) on an HTML answer, a 5xx or a connection error, and reports the outage once. A board call that never answers counts as an outage after 30 s, and the status probe gives up after 5 s. A 4xx (a bad or revoked key) is logged once and the listener goes on, as before: the call answers null. The platform's status file, when it is published, adds a reason and a retry_after. Pia's Stop hook tells a board outage from a listener that is really down: during an outage it does not block the stop, and says the kit retries by itself.
