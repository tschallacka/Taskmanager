- **Crew kit: a board outage is waited out, not a reason to stop.** The listener retries a board call with backoff (60 s, doubling, capped at 5 min) on an HTML answer, a 5xx or a connection error, and reports the outage once. A 4xx is still a fault. The platform's status file, when it is published, adds a reason and a retry_after. Files: `crew/board-retry.mjs`, `crew/board-retry.test.mjs`, `crew/listen.mjs`.
