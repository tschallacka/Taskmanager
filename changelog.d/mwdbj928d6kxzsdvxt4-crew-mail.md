- **The crew's mail goes through the app.** A person's mail is kept by the server until the person's mailer has delivered it, so a mailer on any machine can fetch it once a minute. `POST /api/crew-mail/<slug>` sends (the sender is the caller's own key, never a body field), `GET /api/crew-mail/<slug>?to=<key>` returns the undelivered mail for one person (the boss key may read any), and `POST /api/crew-mail/<slug>/<id>/ack` marks it delivered (only the recipient can). A key of another crew, a customer or a non-crew key gets 403. Text up to 1 MB (the one safety stop; a text is never cut); 30 sends a minute per sender; 500 undelivered per recipient; delivered mail is deleted after 7 days and undelivered after 30. The mailer falls back to its outbox when the endpoint does not answer and flushes it later; a mail is delivered once, even when an ack fails.
