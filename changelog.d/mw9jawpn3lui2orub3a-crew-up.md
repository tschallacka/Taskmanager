- **The crew comes back after a restart of Boss's session.** `crew up` brings the parts in the `up` list of crew.json back in order (already running, started, or failed and why), and is safe to run twice. `crew/detach.sh` keeps Pia's session and the dev services in a detached tmux session, so they outlive the session that started them. `crew restart-brief` lists each standing member's open cards and last update for Pia to start them fresh. `crew pia-session` prints Pia's current session name, and `crew pia-health` says whether her session, listener and transcript are alive; `crew/pia-term.sh` is the fallback to her terminal. Files: `crew/up.mjs`, `crew/detach.sh`, `crew/restart-brief.mjs`, `crew/pia-session.mjs`, `crew/pia-health.mjs`, `crew/pia-term.sh`, `crew/PROMPTS.md.tmpl`, `crew/SKILL.md`, `crew/kit-files.txt`.
